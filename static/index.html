Spatial Measure

Put the printed marker flat on the same surface as what you measure. Capture, tap the points, press Measure.
A4 target: print tools output a4_target.png at 100 %, lay it flat next to or under the objects. Auto outline: tap once inside the object on a plain background.
Known-object mode: first tap its 4 corners in order top-left, top-right, bottom-right, bottom-left (amber), then tap the points to measure (teal).
Volume and weight
Side photo for height: photograph the object from the side with the marker standing on the same vertical plane as its front face (taped to a wall, a box or a book held upright), measure the vertical distance in Length mode, then press Use as: Height. Do the top view for length, width or footprint the same way.
